5. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Create and manage your Plot360 account;
  • Register, list, and enable monitoring of properties you are authorized to submit;
  • Verify identity and ownership/authorization documents (including the Approval Letter and NOC);
  • Communicate with you about your account, listings, and service updates;
  • Maintain the security, integrity, and proper functioning of the Platform;
  • Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and law-enforcement requirements;
  • Improve our services through aggregated or anonymized analysis; and
  • With your separate consent, send you promotional or marketing communications, which you may opt out of at any time.

6. Consent & Legal Basis

We process your personal data primarily on the basis of your consent, given through a clear affirmative action (such as ticking a consent box or submitting a signed Approval Letter/NOC) after being shown an itemized notice describing the personal data to be collected and the purpose of processing, as required under the DPDP Act. You may give, refuse, or withdraw consent for a specific purpose at any time; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal, and may limit your ability to use certain features of the Platform. Certain limited processing (for example, to comply with a legal obligation, or data you voluntarily provide for a stated purpose) may be carried out under the "legitimate uses" recognized by the DPDP Act, without separate consent, as permitted by law.

7. Sharing & Disclosure of Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share personal data with:

  • Service providers who host, store, or process data on our behalf (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics, identity-verification, payment processing), under contractual confidentiality and security obligations;
  • Other users, only to the extent necessary to provide the monitoring/listing service you have requested;
  • Government authorities or regulators, where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Plot360, our users, or the public;
  • A successor entity, in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets, subject to this Policy continuing to apply to your data.

8. Cross-Border Data Transfer

Your data may be processed or stored on servers located outside India. Under the DPDP Act, Plot360 may transfer personal data outside India except to countries specifically restricted by the Central Government. Where such transfers occur, we take reasonable steps to ensure your data continues to receive an appropriate standard of protection.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, or as required by applicable law (including tax, accounting, and regulatory requirements). Where you withdraw consent or request erasure and no legal obligation requires retention, we will delete or anonymize your data within a reasonable time, in accordance with applicable law.

10. Data Security

We implement reasonable security practices and procedures, including access controls, encryption in transit, and internal policies, to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, consistent with the IT (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules, 2011.
